Questions contractors ask us

Don't see yours? Book a demo and ask it live, or call the AI yourself and try to stump it.

Is it cheaper than an answering service or a part-time receptionist?+

Usually by a wide margin. It costs a flat monthly fee, never calls in sick, picks up nights and weekends, and actually books the job instead of just taking a message.

Will customers know they're talking to AI?+

It sounds natural and handles interruptions, accents, and background noise. It can introduce itself as your virtual assistant, and callers can ask for a person at any time.

How does it know what counts as an emergency?+

You set the rules: active leaks, no heat under a certain temperature, burning smells, sewage backups. Emergencies get routed to your on-call tech, everything else gets booked into normal hours.

Which software does it work with?+

It connects to the field service and calendar tools most shops already use, including ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, and Google Calendar. If you run something else, we'll map a workflow during onboarding.

How long does setup take?+

Most shops are live within a week. We load your services, pricing, service area, and hours, then you forward your line or set it to pick up only after hours or on overflow.

What happens with an angry caller or a weird request?+

It stays calm, gets the details, and transfers to you or your office when a human is needed. You get the full transcript and recording either way.

Does it work for multiple trucks or locations?+

Yes. It can route by zip code, trade, or tech availability, and each location gets its own hours and service area.
